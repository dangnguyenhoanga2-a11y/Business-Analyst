# Business Process Analysis for Sales Management at FAHASA

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the sales management process of FAHASA bookstore. The objective is to evaluate the current business processes, identify operational issues, and propose solutions to improve efficiency and customer experience.

The analysis includes both offline and online sales processes and supports the transition toward an Omni-channel business model.

## Objective

* Analyze the current sales management process
* Identify problems and inefficiencies in the workflow
* Design improved business processes
* Improve delivery and customer experience
* Support business decision-making

## Scope

The project analyzes the following processes:

* Offline sales process at bookstore
* Online sales process
* Order processing and payment workflow
* Inventory and delivery coordination
* Customer order handling

## Methodology

Business analysis techniques used in this project:

* BACCM Model
* SWOT Analysis
* SIPOC Model
* BPMN (Business Process Model and Notation)
* Data Flow Diagram (DFD)
* User Story
* Requirement Traceability Matrix (RTM)
* Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## Key Deliverables

This project includes:
